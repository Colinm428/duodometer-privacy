Duodometer — Support

Duodometer is a battery-stats app for the iPhone Duo's dual-battery system, developed by Colin Morgan (Techout).

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: Colin@techoutofficial.com

Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem. Support is provided by email; replies may take a few business days.

Common questions

Why are the per-cell numbers labeled "estimated"?

The iPhone Duo has two battery cells (2,962 mAh in the inner half, 1,921 mAh in the outer half), but iOS manages them as one battery and gives apps only the combined percentage. There is no way for any app to read the individual cells, so Duodometer estimates each cell's charge by applying the combined level to each cell's rated capacity. For measured per-cell health, see Settings → Battery Health on your device.

Why is the level history chart empty?

Duodometer records battery readings only while the app is running. On a fresh install the chart starts empty and fills in as readings are taken. Leave the app open for a while and check back.

The gauge says "Battery level unavailable."

That appears in the iOS Simulator, which has no battery for iOS to report. On a physical iPhone Duo, the level populates normally.

Does Duodometer collect my data?

No. Duodometer collects nothing — no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no network connections. Battery readings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. See the privacy policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone Duo running iOS 27 or later.
