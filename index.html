Duodometer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Duodometer is a battery-stats app for iPhone Duo developed by Colin Morgan (Techout). This policy is short because the app is simple: Duodometer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data — or any data at all.

Data collection

None. Duodometer has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network connections.

Data stored on your device

To show its statistics, Duodometer reads your device's battery level and state from iOS and keeps a history of those readings (battery level samples) in the app's own on-device storage, so it can display a level chart and a drain/charge rate. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Uninstalling is the only "deletion request" mechanism needed, because nothing exists anywhere else.

Data sharing

None. There is nothing to share: no data is collected or transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Children's privacy

Duodometer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Duodometer changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change before that version is released.
